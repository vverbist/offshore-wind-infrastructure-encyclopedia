HVDC and Substation Platforms

Role and Cost Boundary

The offshore platform supports the AC collection equipment, HVDC converter equipment, auxiliaries and accommodation required at sea. This page owns the support structure and foundation cost. Converter electrical equipment and offshore installation are costed separately.

Physical scope Treatment Cost owner
Topside structural steel and outfitting Included here This page
Jacket or other support structure and foundation Included here This page
Converter valves, transformers and electrical auxiliaries Excluded HVDC Converter Stations
Transport, heavy lifts, hookup and offshore commissioning Excluded Platform and Substation Installation

Green = booked on this page Grey = reference only; not booked on this page

Reference 2 GW Facility

TenneT’s indicative 2 GW offshore facility has a topside of roughly 106 m x 77 m x 41 m and about 28,000 t, supported by an approximately 8,000–11,000 t jacket (TenneT 2024). These quantities drive fabrication capacity, load-out, transport, foundation design and offshore lifting.

Available Cost Evidence

The Danish Energy Agency reports the following 2025 station values at the 2020 price level (Danish Energy Agency 2025):

DEA station scope Low Central High
Onshore DC converter station 250 EUR/kW 320 EUR/kW 420 EUR/kW
Offshore DC converter station, platform included 580 EUR/kW 750 EUR/kW 980 EUR/kW

Restated to constant 2026 euros with the common 1.263 factor, the central values are 404 EUR/kW for the onshore station and 948 EUR/kW for the offshore station including its platform. The difference is 544 EUR/kW.

This is useful as an offshore platform and offshore-premium proxy, but it is not a clean platform quotation. It may contain platform steel, foundation, offshore buildings, marinisation, different electrical scope and other offshore cost.

The internal model description uses 750 EUR/kW for “platform and installation” (Project Team 2026). It does not separate platform fabrication from transport and installation.

Cost Model and Context

Cost-stack element Reference value Status Cost owner
Converter pair, platform excluded 808 EUR/kW Adopted on linked page HVDC Converter Stations
Offshore platform and offshore premium proxy 544 EUR/kW DEA difference; context only This page
Legacy platform + installation allocation 750 EUR/kW Combined scope; context only This page and Platform Installation
Adopted standalone platform CAPEX TBD Requires platform-only evidence or mass-based model This page
Offshore platform installation TBD Costed separately Platform and Substation Installation
WarningDo not add the proxy and the legacy allocation

The 544 EUR/kW DEA difference and the 750 EUR/kW internal allocation are alternative scope indicators. Neither is currently an adopted standalone platform cost, and they must not be added together.

The next defensible improvement is a mass-based platform model calibrated against a platform-only fabrication benchmark. Until then, the page exposes the available evidence without assigning false precision to the split.

Energy and Availability

The support structure has no direct conversion loss. Platform common-mode events may remove some or all converter capacity; these effects belong in the station availability-state model rather than a separate flat platform factor.

Model Limitations

Limitation Implication
DEA offshore value includes converter equipment Its difference from an onshore station is only a platform proxy
Internal value combines platform and installation Fabrication and marine installation cannot be booked independently
No mass-based cost function Changes in topside or jacket mass are not represented
Foundation type and water depth are not parameterised Site-specific structural cost is absent
