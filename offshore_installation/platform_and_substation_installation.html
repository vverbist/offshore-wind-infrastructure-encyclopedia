Platform and Substation Installation

Role in the Model

This page calculates the offshore installation CAPEX of a conventionally crane-installed, piled-jacket offshore substation (OSS). The result is needed to convert the separately costed jacket, piles and topside into an installed offshore asset without hiding transport, vessel occupation or commissioning inside the fabrication cost.

The calculation separates four work packages:

  1. foundation piles and, for pre-piling, the positioning template;
  2. jacket transport, lift, set-down and structural connection;
  3. topside transport, lift, set-down and structural connection; and
  4. offshore hook-up, testing and commissioning.

These packages may share a mobilisation or vessel in a project execution plan, but they do not share a universal rate or productivity. The model is not applicable without a method change to float-over topsides, gravity bases, self-installing platforms or ultra-heavy single-lift systems.

ImportantFeasibility precedes cost

A lift or transport arrangement that fails a capacity, geometry or stability check is not represented by adding installation days. The model must select another vessel class or installation method before calculating duration and cost.

Installation Accounting

The model uses physical units that remain distinct throughout the calculation.

Quantity Definition Modelling consequence
Substation platform One functional OSS at one location Contains one or more lift modules and normally one jacket
Jacket leg One main structural leg Does not by itself determine pile count
Foundation pile One driven pile A leg may have one or several piles
Pre-piling template Reusable positioning frame installed before the piles Set and recovered per location, not per pile
Topside module One lift unit in the execution plan A split topside creates additional lifts and offshore interfaces
Carrier load One jacket or topside delivery from the yard or port Large structures default to one per load until a stow plan proves otherwise
Heavy lift One crane pick at a specified mass, radius and lift path Brochure maximum crane capacity is insufficient evidence
Net installation day Chargeable work time excluding defined long interruptions Is not the full calendar commissioning period
Campaign A continuous deployment sharing mobilisation Pile, jacket, topside and commissioning campaigns may differ

Every numerical model input must be recorded as a sourced input, explicit modelling assumption or derived result. A missing required engineering or commercial input blocks the affected cost result.

Lift and Transport Feasibility

For lift unit \(j\), the crane-capacity gate is

\[ \gamma_{\mathrm{dyn},j}\left(m_j+m_{\mathrm{rig},j}\right) \leq SWL(R_j,\mathrm{config}_j), \]

with the geometry gates

\[ H_{\mathrm{hook}} \geq H_{\mathrm{required},j}, \qquad R_{\mathrm{available}} \geq R_j. \]

For the selected installation vessel or deck carrier,

\[ m_j+m_{\mathrm{seafast},j}\leq M_{\mathrm{use}}, \qquad \frac{B_jL_j}{f_{\mathrm{pack}}}\leq A_{\mathrm{use}}, \qquad p_{\mathrm{local},j}\leq p_{\mathrm{deck,max}}. \]

Here \(m_j\), \(m_{\mathrm{rig},j}\) and \(m_{\mathrm{seafast},j}\) are the lift-unit, rigging and seafastening masses in tonnes; \(\gamma_{\mathrm{dyn},j}\) is the dimensionless engineered dynamic factor; \(SWL\) is the crane safe working load in tonnes at radius \(R_j\) and the stated configuration; \(H_{\mathrm{hook}}\) and \(H_{\mathrm{required},j}\) are available and required hook heights in metres; \(B_j\) and \(L_j\) are cargo dimensions in metres; \(f_{\mathrm{pack}}\) is the usable packing fraction; \(M_{\mathrm{use}}\) and \(A_{\mathrm{use}}\) are usable carrier mass and area; and \(p_{\mathrm{local},j}\) and \(p_{\mathrm{deck,max}}\) are actual and allowable local deck loads.

The engineered check must also cover centre of gravity, lifting-point loads, tandem synchronisation, boom and structure clearance, under-hook rigging, carrier stability, quay load-out, water depth, vessel draft, air gap and DP or mooring footprint.

Public fleet specifications illustrate discontinuous capability classes rather than default vessel choices: Rambiz and Gulliver are reported at 3,300 t and 4,000 t tandem capacity (Scaldis Salvage and Marine Contractors, n.d.); Saipem 7000 at 14,000 t combined capacity (Saipem, n.d.); and Sleipnir at 20,000 t tandem capacity with a 220 m by 102 m reinforced deck (Heerema Marine Contractors, n.d.). Pioneering Spirit reports separate 60,000 t topside and 20,000 t jacket systems (Allseas, n.d.). Actual radius, lift path and configuration always govern.

Dependency-Ordered Calculation

Physical Inventory

For uniform platforms,

\[ N_{\mathrm{pile}} =N_{\mathrm{OSS}}N_{\mathrm{leg}}N_{\mathrm{pile/leg}}, \]

\[ N_{\mathrm{lift},J}=N_{\mathrm{OSS}}, \qquad N_{\mathrm{lift},S}=N_{\mathrm{OSS}}N_{\mathrm{module/OSS}}. \]

Here all \(N\) terms are counts: substations, legs, piles, jackets or topside modules as indicated by their subscripts. Designs that differ between locations are calculated individually and summed.

Carrier loads are

\[ N_{\mathrm{load},J} =\left\lceil\frac{N_{\mathrm{lift},J}}{n_{J/\mathrm{load}}}\right\rceil, \qquad N_{\mathrm{load},S} =\left\lceil\frac{N_{\mathrm{lift},S}}{n_{S/\mathrm{load}}}\right\rceil, \]

where \(n_{J/\mathrm{load}}\) and \(n_{S/\mathrm{load}}\) are structures per proven carrier load. The screening default is one large jacket or topside per load, consistent with the public CVOW construction envelope (Dominion Energy 2021).

Pile and Template Campaign

For a pre-piled jacket location,

\[ \begin{aligned} t_{P,\mathrm{OSS}}={}&t_{\mathrm{pos},P}+t_{\mathrm{template,set}}\\ &+N_{\mathrm{pile/OSS}}\left( t_{\mathrm{release},p} +\frac{L_p}{v_{\mathrm{upend},p}} +\frac{d_{\mathrm{lower},p}}{v_{\mathrm{lower},p}} +t_{\mathrm{stab},p} +\frac{L_e}{q_{\mathrm{drive,eff}}} +t_{\mathrm{survey},p} \right)\\ &+t_{\mathrm{template,recover}}. \end{aligned} \]

\(t_{P,\mathrm{OSS}}\) is net pile-campaign time in days per platform. The component \(t\) terms are task durations converted to days; \(L_p\) and \(L_e\) are pile length and embedded penetration in metres; \(d_{\mathrm{lower},p}\) is lowering distance in metres; \(v_{\mathrm{upend},p}\) and \(v_{\mathrm{lower},p}\) are handling speeds in metres per day; and \(q_{\mathrm{drive,eff}}\) is the site-specific effective penetration rate in metres per day for the selected pile-soil-hammer system.

Total pile-campaign occupation is

\[ T_P=t_{\mathrm{setup},P} +\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\mathrm{OSS}}}t_{P,i} +T_{\mathrm{transit},P} +T_{\mathrm{loadout},P} +T_{\mathrm{fieldmove},P}, \]

where every \(T\) and \(t\) term is in days. For post-piling, the jacket is installed first and piles are handled through its sleeves. The combined jacket-vessel and piling-spread critical path must then replace the pre-pile template sequence. Relief drilling, pile refusal and extraordinary noise restrictions are not included in the normal penetration rate.

Jacket Campaign

Net jacket time per location is

\[ t_{J,\mathrm{OSS}} =t_{\mathrm{pos},J}+t_{\mathrm{carrier,moor}} +t_{\mathrm{release},J} +\frac{H_{\mathrm{path},J}}{v_{\mathrm{crane},J}} +t_{\mathrm{set},J}+t_{\mathrm{level},J} +t_{\mathrm{connect},J}+t_{\mathrm{survey},J} +t_{\mathrm{rig,release},J}. \]

\(H_{\mathrm{path},J}\) is the jacket hook travel in metres, \(v_{\mathrm{crane},J}\) is the effective hoisting or lowering speed in metres per day, and all other terms are task durations in days. The structural-connection term includes only the selected grout, weld or mechanical interface. The method statement must say whether cure and inspection occupy the heavy-lift vessel.

Topside Campaign

For each topside lift,

\[ t_{S,j} =t_{\mathrm{pos},S,j}+t_{\mathrm{carrier,moor},j} +t_{\mathrm{release},S,j} +\frac{H_{\mathrm{path},S,j}}{v_{\mathrm{crane},S,j}} +t_{\mathrm{mate},S,j}+t_{\mathrm{connect},S,j} +t_{\mathrm{survey},S,j}+t_{\mathrm{rig,release},S,j}. \]

The terms have the same units and meanings as in the jacket equation, with \(S\) identifying the topside. Total topside site time is \(T_{S,\mathrm{site}}=\sum_j t_{S,j}\). A split topside is calculated module by module and includes its additional module-to-module connections. No universal linear tonnes-to-hours relationship is used.

Transport and Vessel Concurrency

For installation class \(c\in\{J,S\}\) with one round trip per carrier load,

\[ T_{\mathrm{carrier},c} =N_{\mathrm{load},c} \left( t_{\mathrm{load},c} +\frac{2D_p}{v_{\mathrm{carrier},c}} +t_{\mathrm{offload},c} \right), \]

where \(D_p\) is the one-way port or fabrication-yard distance in kilometres, \(v_{\mathrm{carrier},c}\) is transit speed in kilometres per day, and the resulting duration is in days.

Heavy-lift-vessel occupation is

\[ T_{\mathrm{HLV},c} =t_{\mathrm{setup},c} +T_{c,\mathrm{site}} +T_{\mathrm{HLV,transit},c} +T_{\mathrm{wait,critical},c}. \]

Carrier and HLV durations are calculated and costed separately because they often overlap. Carrier time is added to the HLV critical path only when the schedule demonstrates a supply bottleneck. A simple one-structure-per-load capacity check is

\[ Q_c =\min\left( \frac{1}{t_{\mathrm{site},c}}, \frac{n_{\mathrm{carrier},c}}{t_{\mathrm{carrier\ cycle},c}} \right), \]

where \(Q_c\) is achievable structures per day, \(n_{\mathrm{carrier},c}\) is the number of carriers, and both cycle times are in days per structure.

Hook-Up and Commissioning

The separate commissioning campaign is

\[ T_H =\frac{N_{\mathrm{OSS}} \left(t_{\mathrm{mechanical}}+t_{\mathrm{electrical}} +t_{\mathrm{controls}}+t_{\mathrm{test}}\right)} {n_{\mathrm{parallel},H}} +t_{\mathrm{setup},H}, \]

where \(T_H\) and all task terms are in days and \(n_{\mathrm{parallel},H}\) is the number of simultaneous work fronts. The selected accommodation and commissioning spread is costed for this duration. The HLV is included only if the execution plan retains it.

Cost Calculation

For each package \(c\in\{P,J,S,H\}\),

\[ C_c =C_{\mathrm{mob},c} +r_{\mathrm{primary},c}T_{\mathrm{primary},c} +r_{\mathrm{carrier},c}T_{\mathrm{carrier},c} +\sum_v r_{\mathrm{support},v}T_v +C_{\mathrm{consumables},c}. \]

Total installation CAPEX is

\[ C_{\mathrm{OSS}} =\sum_{c\in\{P,J,S,H\}}C_c +C_{\mathrm{exceptional}} +C_{\mathrm{other}}. \]

Symbol Definition Unit
\(C_c\) Cost of work package \(c\) 2026 EUR
\(C_{\mathrm{mob},c}\) Mobilisation and demobilisation of the package spread 2026 EUR/campaign
\(r_{\mathrm{primary},c}\), \(r_{\mathrm{carrier},c}\), \(r_{\mathrm{support},v}\) Day rate for the named primary vessel, carrier or support vessel 2026 EUR/day
\(T_{\mathrm{primary},c}\), \(T_{\mathrm{carrier},c}\), \(T_v\) Chargeable occupation of the corresponding vessel day
\(C_{\mathrm{consumables},c}\) Explicit package consumables not included in a vessel or spread rate 2026 EUR
\(C_{\mathrm{exceptional}}\) Separately enumerated exceptional work 2026 EUR
\(C_{\mathrm{other}}\) Other named installation cost not included elsewhere 2026 EUR
\(C_{\mathrm{OSS}}\) Total offshore-substation installation CAPEX 2026 EUR

If a complete contracted spread rate \(r_{\mathrm{spread},c}\) is available, the package calculation becomes \(C_c=C_{\mathrm{mob},c}+r_{\mathrm{spread},c}T_{\mathrm{spread},c}+C_{\mathrm{consumables},c}\). The vessel terms already included in that spread are omitted.

Evidence-Graded Screening Inputs

Parameter Screening treatment Evidence and limitation
Piled-jacket net site occupation 5.0 day/OSS typical anchor CVOW planning envelope; calculate from activities when inputs are available (Dominion Energy 2021)
Topside net placement 2.5 day/lift typical anchor Placement and mating only; excludes full hook-up and commissioning (Dominion Energy 2021)
Structures per carrier load 1 large jacket or topside/load Conservative CVOW planning treatment; replace with an engineered stow plan (Dominion Energy 2021)
Generic moderate-HLV proxy 289,717 EUR/day Inferred from an ORBIT output of USD 936,918 for 72 h and normalized from 2024 USD; not a quote or complete spread (National Laboratory of the Rockies 2026)
HLV mobilisation 3 day proxy; 7 day contextual case Three days inferred from the ORBIT event; seven days is a historical NREL model assumption (National Laboratory of the Rockies 2026; Shields et al. 2021)
Carrier rate Supplier input required No current, consistently scoped public rate identified
Piling spread and penetration Contractor and geotechnical input required Depends on the pile-soil-hammer system
Hook-up duration and spread rate Owner/EPCI and contractor inputs required Must not be inferred from topside lift time

The 5.0-day and 2.5-day values are planning anchors, not probability-distribution centres with invented bounds. A favourable case is supported by an activity calculation below the anchor; a difficult case is produced by its physical drivers above the anchor. A second topside module, relief drilling, float-over or ultra-heavy lift is a model branch, not a generic multiplier.

The generic HLV proxy is suitable only for a transparent screening sensitivity in a moderate vessel class. It is not adopted automatically for a multi-thousand-tonne HVDC jacket or topside. Until named-vessel rates and complete scope boundaries are supplied, the model reports vessel-days and a cost sensitivity rather than an adopted installation CAPEX.

Exceptional Work

Exceptional work is calculated outside normal campaign productivity:

\[ \begin{aligned} C_{\mathrm{exceptional}}={}& N_{\mathrm{drill}}r_{\mathrm{drill}}t_{\mathrm{drill}} +N_{\mathrm{refusal}}C_{\mathrm{remediate}} +N_{\mathrm{noise}}C_{\mathrm{noise}}\\ &+A_{\mathrm{prep}}c_{\mathrm{seabed}} +A_{\mathrm{scour}}c_{\mathrm{scour}} +C_{\mathrm{UXO}}+C_{\mathrm{weather\ restriction}} +C_{\mathrm{exceptional,other}}. \end{aligned} \]

\(N_{\mathrm{drill}}\), \(N_{\mathrm{refusal}}\) and \(N_{\mathrm{noise}}\) are event counts; \(r_{\mathrm{drill}}\) is a 2026-EUR/day drilling-spread rate; \(t_{\mathrm{drill}}\) is days per drilled pile; \(A_{\mathrm{prep}}\) and \(A_{\mathrm{scour}}\) are treated areas in square metres; the corresponding lowercase \(c\) terms are 2026-EUR/m2 unit costs; and the remaining uppercase \(C\) terms are 2026-EUR lump sums. Each term is site-specific unless a contract explicitly includes it elsewhere.

Constant-2026-EUR Normalization

Each source cost retains its own currency, price year, index and geographic adjustment:

\[ C_{2026,\mathrm{EUR}} =C_{y,s} \frac{I_s(2026^*)}{I_s(y)} FX_{\mathrm{EUR}/s}(2026^*) F_{\mathrm{geo}}, \]

where \(C_{y,s}\) is a cost in source currency \(s\) and year \(y\); \(I_s\) is the chosen escalation index; \(FX_{\mathrm{EUR}/s}\) is euros per unit of source currency at the stated reference date; and \(F_{\mathrm{geo}}\) is a separately documented dimensionless market factor.

For the 2024-USD ORBIT proxy, annual-average 2024 CPI-U of 313.689, June 2026 CPI-U of 333.952 and the 30 July 2026 ECB rate of USD 1.1476 per EUR give (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics 2026b, 2026a; European Central Bank 2026)

\[ F_{2024\,\mathrm{USD}\rightarrow2026\,\mathrm{EUR}} =\frac{333.952}{313.689}\frac{1}{1.1476} =0.9276715\ \mathrm{EUR}_{2026}/\mathrm{USD}_{2024}. \]

CPI is a transparent fallback, not an offshore-vessel market index. No geographic adjustment is embedded in this factor.

A EUR/kW value may be reported only as a derived comparison after total installation CAPEX has been calculated. It is not a substitute for the quantities and vessel-days above.

Validation Cases and Assertions

Public projects test selected parts of the model; they do not supply a complete transferable cost coefficient.

Case Public information used Model check Limitation
CVOW About 5 net days per piled jacket, 2.5 net days per topside and one large structure per carrier load (Dominion Energy 2021) Site duration and carrier count Planning envelope, not measured cost; commissioning is longer
Butendiek 1,032 t jacket, four 238 t piles and 2,250 t topside (Scaldis Salvage and Marine Contractors 2014) Pile count, lift class and activity completeness No disclosed vessel-days or package price
Rampion Approximately 2,000 t topside installed by Rambiz (Rampion Offshore Wind Farm, n.d.) Crane-class margin No complete jacket or spread-cost record
Dogger Bank A/B Approximately 2,900–3,100 t jackets and 8,500 t topsides (Saipem 2020, 2023) Heavy-lift class change No auditable public rates or durations
Dogger Bank C Approximately 3,500 t jacket and 9,500 t topside (Dogger Bank Wind Farm, n.d.) Heavy-lift class change Contract announcement, not installation cost evidence
ORBIT generic HLV output USD 936,918 mobilisation cost for 72 h (National Laboratory of the Rockies 2026) Reproduction of the rate proxy and currency conversion Generic model output, not a jacket-specific quote

The implementation must enforce these assertions:

  • every lift and carrier gate passes before time and cost are calculated;
  • every pile, lift and carrier load is counted from the physical design;
  • pre-pile and post-pile logic are not combined accidentally;
  • concurrent carrier and HLV days are costed independently but not serially added without a demonstrated critical-path effect;
  • hook-up and commissioning are not charged at an HLV rate unless the vessel is retained;
  • a complete spread and its constituent vessels are not both charged;
  • exceptional work appears exactly once; and
  • every cost input has a currency, price year, normalization record and explicit scope.

Limitations and Required Evidence

Unresolved evidence Consequence
Named-vessel rate, mobilisation, minimum charter and inclusions for each campaign No adopted commercial installation CAPEX
Safe working load at the engineered radius, rigging and dynamic factor Lift feasibility remains open
Carrier stow plan, load-out and seafastening details Carrier count and cycle remain screening values
Site-specific pile handling and effective penetration Pile duration cannot be closed bottom-up
Critical-path connection, grout cure and inspection time Jacket-vessel occupation remains uncertain
Offshore completion scope, parallel work fronts and accommodation spread Hook-up and commissioning cost remains open
Relief drilling, noise, seabed, scour and UXO quantities and rates Exceptional-work allowance cannot be calculated

The current evidence supports an auditable calculation structure, feasibility gates and limited screening sensitivities. It does not support concealing the unresolved inputs inside a platform-and-installation EUR/kW allocation.
