Financial and Price-Basis Assumptions

Role in the Model

This page defines the common monetary basis, lifecycle capital basis and annual cost calculation. Component pages retain ownership of source values, component-specific scaling, installation boundaries, replacement scope and annual OPEX.

Canonical Monetary Basis

All costs entering a common model result are expressed in constant 2025 euros (EUR2025). Each imported cost retains its source currency, source price year and cost boundary.

A value with an unresolved price year cannot be adopted as an EUR2025 model input. It may be shown only as a non-combinable reference until the evidence gap is resolved.

No generic geographic, offshore or market multiplier is applied. Such a factor may be introduced only where evidence defines its scope and prevents overlap with component, installation or commercial allowances.

Currency and Price-Year Normalization

A source cost in currency \(s\) and price year \(y\) is normalized as:

\[ C_{2025,\mathrm{EUR}} = C_{y,s} \frac{I_{s,k}(2025)}{I_{s,k}(y)} FX_{\mathrm{EUR}/s,2025} G_{\mathrm{market}}. \]

Symbol Definition Unit or status
\(C_{y,s}\) Source cost in source currency \(s\) and price year \(y\) currency \(s\)
\(I_{s,k}(t)\) Price index for cost type \(k\) in source market \(s\) index
\(FX_{\mathrm{EUR}/s,2025}\) 2025 annual-average euros per unit of source currency EUR/currency \(s\)
\(G_{\mathrm{market}}\) Separately evidenced geographic-market adjustment dimensionless; default 1.0
\(C_{2025,\mathrm{EUR}}\) Cost on the common model basis EUR2025

Escalation is performed in the source currency before currency conversion. A cost-specific index is preferred where its scope matches the equipment or activity. A consumer-price index may be used only as a transparent fallback, with the mismatch recorded as a limitation.

For USD-to-EUR conversions, the common convention is the ECB 2025 annual average of 1 EUR = 1.1300 USD. The inverse rate is 0.8850 EUR/USD (European Central Bank 2026). A dated exchange rate is not mixed with annual-average price indices in an EUR2025 model input.

The same 6.6%/year real WACC applies to all three architectures. Tax, debt, construction-finance timing and architecture-specific financing are not modelled.

Capital Recovery Factor

\[ CRF = \frac{r(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n-1}. \]

For a real WACC of 6.6%/year and a project life of 25 years, the resulting capital recovery factor is 8.274%/year.

Component Replacement

Replacement is included only when a component owner documents:

  1. a material technical life shorter than 25 years;
  2. the replaced scope;
  3. the replacement cost; and
  4. that continued operation requires the replacement.

For such a component:

\[ N_{\mathrm{replacement},j} = \left\lceil \frac{n}{n_j} \right\rceil-1. \]

A replacement at the end of the 25 years project life is not counted. Components with a technical life of at least 25 years have zero replacement. A short nominal life does not create a replacement cost unless the component page defines the event and boundary.

Total replacement cost is:

\[ C_{\mathrm{replacement}} = \sum_j N_{\mathrm{replacement},j} C_{\mathrm{replacement},j}. \]

Replacement timing is not discounted under the approved screening convention.

Decommissioning

The model does not use a separate decommissioning activity model:

\[ C_{\mathrm{decommissioning}} = f_{\mathrm{decommissioning}} C_{\mathrm{offshore\ installation}}. \]

The offshore-installation total must contain non-overlapping installation costs from the installation pages. The factor \(f_{\mathrm{decommissioning}}\) is 80% and is an explicit modelling assumption, not a sourced contractor estimate. Decommissioning timing is not discounted.

Lifecycle Capital Basis and Annual Cost

\[ C_{\mathrm{lifecycle\ capital}} = C_{\mathrm{initial}} + C_{\mathrm{replacement}} + C_{\mathrm{decommissioning}}. \]

\[ C_{\mathrm{annual}} = CRF C_{\mathrm{lifecycle\ capital}} + \sum_j O_j. \]

Every \(O_j\) is an annual, non-overlapping component OPEX term in EUR2025/year.

The shared project-cost record also contains development, shared engineering, insurance and owner contingency. These fields remain unresolved rather than being set to zero. A complete total-system result is not parameterized until all required fields have adopted values or an explicit scope decision.

Manufactured-Cost Markup

The 1.5 factor used for selected NREL PEM stack, balance-of-plant and power-electronics inputs is not a general financial escalation factor:

\[ C_{\mathrm{purchased}} = k_{\mathrm{markup}}C_{\mathrm{manufactured}}. \]

The factor \(k_{\mathrm{markup}}\) is 1.5 and is an explicit component-level modelling assumption. Badgett et al. document the manufactured-cost boundary but do not provide or validate the markup (Badgett et al. 2024). The component owner must prevent the markup from being applied to procurement or installed-system prices and must keep installation, offshore packaging, soft costs and inflation separate.

Required Checks Before Aggregation

Before costs are added:

  1. source currency, price year and scope are known;
  2. adopted values are normalized to EUR2025;
  3. component, installation and project boundaries do not overlap;
  4. required unresolved fields return not parameterized;
  5. replacement is counted only under the documented rule;
  6. decommissioning is applied once to total offshore installation CAPEX; and
  7. annual OPEX is not embedded in CAPEX or another component term.
