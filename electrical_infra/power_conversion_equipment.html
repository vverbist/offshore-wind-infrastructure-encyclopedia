Power Conversion Equipment

Role in the Model

This page owns the inventory of power-conversion equipment between the turbine generator and the PEM stack DC terminals. For each equipment role it records the duty, voltage and insulation class, documented unit ratings, the unit count implied by the article reference case, cost evidence and its price basis, losses, package boundary and adoption status.

Other pages apply this inventory rather than repeat it. DC Integration defines the turbine-level electrical cases, Electrolyser Power Electronics books the central transformer–rectifier package, Wind Turbine Mass and Cost owns the turbine removal credit, and Component Mapping records which roles each architecture uses.

Candidate evidence on this page is not an adopted model input. A value enters model_data/inputs.csv only after its scope, price basis and applicability are adopted on the owning page. Unsupported values stay as explicit TODOs.

Electrical Chains Compared

Three article cases are compared. The adopted power-curve boundary is the inverter AC output, before the turbine transformer, as defined on Turbine Power Electronics. The existing common operating case still documents generator output; reconciliation of that source curve remains required before numerical use of the new boundary.

Centralised:   generator -> generator-side converter -> DC link -> grid-side inverter
               -> turbine transformer -> 66 kV collection -> central step-down transformer
               -> rectifier transformer -> rectifier -> stacks
Conservative:  generator -> generator-side converter -> DC link -> stack-side DC/DC -> stacks
Direct DC:     generator -> generator-side converter -> DC link -> stacks

The generator-side converter is present in every case. It is therefore excluded from the comparison cost table and stays in the common turbine baseline. At the adopted inverter-output boundary its losses are already upstream of the power curve; no extra generator-side loss or credit is applied when comparing architectures (see Losses). Auxiliary supplies, protection, switchgear and dedicated cooling are named separately in each role below, so that a package boundary cannot silently absorb or drop them.

Reference-Case Unit Counts

The article case has 134 turbines of 15 MW (2010 MW). Counts per turbine follow from the chains above; central counts depend on a selected unit size, which is not yet chosen.

NREL/WISDEM’s cost-model example exposes a converter category, but does not establish a separately priced grid-side inverter or its efficiency (WISDEM Development Team, n.d.). A complete converter cost cannot be deducted when the rectifier remains. The DEA offshore-wind technology brief reports project-level investment categories, including turbines, array cables and substations; it does not provide the inverter-only specifications needed here (Danish Energy Agency 2025). These sources support checking package boundaries, not a numerical inverter credit. The blank central inputs above preserve that evidence gap rather than assigning an unsupported split of a package value.

Losses

For a curve reconciled to inverter AC output, generator and rectifier losses are already included. Centralised hydrogen and electricity export apply the turbine transformer efficiency downstream; decentralised hydrogen divides curve power by inverter efficiency to recover the removed inverter loss. These equations are implemented, but operation requires wind.power_boundary = "inverter_ac_output" after evidence-based reconciliation. The supplied generator-output record remains explicitly unreconciled; changing its label alone is not a boundary conversion. The required terms are:

Loss terms by case and their current treatment.
Loss term Cases Current model treatment Status
Generator-side converter All Legacy source-curve boundary unresolved Included upstream in a reconciled inverter-output curve; no additional architecture loss
Grid-side inverter Included in AC cases; removed in turbine-level cases Recovery by division implemented TODO: inverter-only efficiency and curve reconciliation
Turbine step-up transformer Centralised Downstream multiplier implemented turbine-transformer-efficiency remains TODO
66 kV AC collection Centralised Fixed fraction of aggregate transformer output, before central conversion array-ac-loss-fraction remains TODO; no resistance or power-factor input required
Central transformers and rectifier Centralised 97.5% net package efficiency Adopted
Stack-side DC/DC converter Conservative Blank interface efficiency Candidates above 97.5%
Direct-DC interface Direct DC Blank interface efficiency Should cover only DC-link, protection and cabling losses; no converter loss

O2c must reconcile the curve boundary and close these terms before comparable energy results are reported. No separate generator or rectifier loss is added to the adopted curve. A detailed loss calculation replaces a flat efficiency rather than being added to it.

Cost and Mass Reconciliation

Each electrical function is counted once:

  • The legacy common turbine calibration includes the complete conventional turbine electrical scope, including converter and transformer. The turbine-level cases adjust it through turbine-electrical-cost-adjustment on Wind Turbine Mass and Cost.
  • Under the adopted boundary, the calibrated transformer term moves out of turbine cost and into electrical infrastructure for the AC architectures. The executable ledger makes this reallocation using the original RNA calibration factor: turbine / supply excludes the transformer and AC cases add one turbine-transformer / supply entry. Combined AC purchase cost is unchanged.
  • The transformer removal credit can follow the explicit WISDEM transformer term in that ledger. No grid-side inverter credit is claimed, because the calibrated cost has no separable converter term and no separable evidence exists.
  • The stack-side DC/DC converter is booked through the turbine-level conversion unit cost on DC Integration, not in the turbine adjustment.
  • The central transformers and rectifier are booked in the central package; the platform page carries their mass but not their cost.
  • No mass or cost saving is assumed beyond these explicit terms. Equipment masses for the platform (O3) and turbine lifts (O8) remain inputs; the ABB converter catalogue gives about 6.2 t for a 10 MW converter as context only (ABB 2023).
