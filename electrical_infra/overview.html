Electrical Infrastructure

Purpose and System Boundary

This page shows how the electrical-infrastructure pages fit together and where each assumption is maintained. It does not introduce separate component assumptions.

The electrical chain begins after the turbine inverter and before the turbine step-up transformer. For electricity export, it ends at the onshore grid connection point. The reference architecture uses 66 kV AC array collection and a 525 kV HVDC export system based on TenneT’s 2 GW standard (TenneT 2024, 2025).
