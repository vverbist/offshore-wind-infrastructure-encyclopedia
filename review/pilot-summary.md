# Pilot review summary — ELEC-002

The pilot applied the full review method to
[Infield AC Cables](../electrical_infra/infield_ac_cables.qmd).

## Outcome

- **Page readiness:** blocked
- **Blockers:** 4
- **Major findings:** 8
- **Moderate findings:** 2
- **Decisions raised:** 2

The review did not change the source page. Findings are stored in
`issue-register.csv`, supporting input and interface records are stored in the
two technical registers, and the detailed reasoning is in
`page-reviews/ELEC-002.md`.

## What the pilot demonstrated

The review rubric successfully separated:

- correct and useful existing material;
- explicit limitations already disclosed by the author;
- new implementation gaps;
- cross-page ownership conflicts; and
- evidence-maintenance problems that do not currently change the calculation.

This distinction is important. An explicit limitation is still a model blocker,
but it should not be reported as though the review newly discovered it.

The pilot also showed that page review must include at least three linked pages:
the upstream quantity owner, the downstream consumer and the shared methodology
owner. A page can look internally complete while its output is unusable or its
excluded cost has no owner elsewhere.

## Framework adjustment

The issue register now includes `finding_type`, `resolution` and
`validation_evidence` fields. These additions preserve whether a finding was
explicit or newly discovered and provide a defensible closure record after
correction.

No further rubric change is recommended before the remaining review batches.
