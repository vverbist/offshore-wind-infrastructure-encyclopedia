## Executive assessment

The centralised 66 kV-to-stack-DC boundary, reference topology and linear
module scaling are explained well. The adopted 185 EUR/kW cost has no common
price year and differs from the executable 100 per kW assumption. The page
uses 97.5% efficiency while code uses 98.5%. Equipment mass is absent, which
also prevents closure of the central platform inventory.

## 1. Model role and boundary

- **Purpose:** represent the complete centralised 66 kV AC-to-stack-DC package.
- **Included:** plant/rectifier transformers, rectifiers, controls, protection,
  harmonic provision and dedicated cooling.
- **Excluded:** upstream 66 kV switchgear, turbine equipment, stack, platform
  auxiliaries and installation.
- **Ownership concern:** upstream switchgear remains unowned (ISS-0012).

## 3. Calculation reconstruction

CAPEX is installed kW × 185 EUR/kW with linear scaling. Stack DC power is 97.5%
of 66 kV input at every load. Availability is delegated to an overall
electrolyser factor. The relationships are executable but conflict with the
legacy parameter file and do not generate mass or block counts.

### Boundary and limiting-case checks

- Zero capacity gives zero cost.
- Repeated block count/redundancy should be discrete even if cost is linear.
- Fixed efficiency must stay between zero and one; low-load sensitivity is
  needed for oversizing cases.
- Transformer ownership must be unique.

## 5. Technical correctness

A 24-pulse thyristor reference and approximately linear numbering-up are
reasonable screening choices. A fixed net efficiency is acceptable with a
sensitivity. The chosen value must be single and code-aligned. The page's
97.5% and code's 98.5% differ by about 1% of input energy, material for LCOH.

## 6. Model fidelity

Aggregating the complete conversion package is the right fidelity. Explicit
block counts, redundancy and mass are needed because platform feasibility and
partial outages depend on them; a detailed converter circuit model is not.

## 7. Cost and evidence audit

The literature triangulation is useful, but sources with different years,
currencies, regions and commercial boundaries are compared at face value. The
central 185 EUR/kW value cannot enter the EUR2025 ledger until its source year
and normalisation are fixed. Legacy CAPEX is 100,000 per MW and therefore not
the documented model.

## 8. Independent-implementer test

The page formula is implementable, but the current repository cannot identify
which cost/efficiency is authoritative and cannot pass equipment mass or block
state downstream.

## 9. Explanation and site placement

Clear, direct and correctly placed. Add an adopted-input box and link the
shared availability owner after reconciliation.
