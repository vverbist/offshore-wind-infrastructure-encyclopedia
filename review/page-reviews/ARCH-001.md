## Assessment

The page clearly distinguishes the three architectures by infield carrier,
conversion location, export carrier, platform presence and onshore connection.
The flow diagram and comparison table are consistent with the individual
architecture pages. Placement immediately before those pages is logical, all
links resolve, and the prose is direct.

The page is especially useful in separating the physical energy-flow boundary
from the economic lifecycle boundary. It also makes the one-platform central
hydrogen and no-central-platform decentralised assumptions explicit.

Two cross-page gaps prevent the overview from describing a complete comparison:

- the stated delivery points are different products and exclude downstream
  functions needed for a common end-use comparison (ISS-0015);
- several lifecycle categories said to be inside the economic boundary have no
  canonical owner or parameterisation (ISS-0017 and ISS-0030).

The phrases "replacement where represented" and "decommissioning where
represented" correctly avoid claiming that those cash flows are complete, but
they also leave the economic boundary conditional. Once the project cost ledger
is decided, the overview should state included, excluded and unresolved scopes
without the conditional wording.
