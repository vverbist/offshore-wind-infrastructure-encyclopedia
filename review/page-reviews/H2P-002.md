## Executive assessment

The page gives a careful, technically credible conditional description of
direct DC coupling and correctly reallocates rather than deletes converter
functions. It cannot yet demonstrate a feasible operating envelope and has no
quantitative ledger for retained rectifier/up-chopper, protection, isolation,
losses, mass or cost. The legacy yield model additionally multiplies wind
power by an undocumented 1.025 “electric gain”; that is not the page's stated
energy balance and can create energy above the defined generator output.

## 1. Model role and boundary

- **Purpose:** define generator-to-PEM electrical coupling for decentralised
  hydrogen.
- **Included:** generator-side rectifier/up-chopper, DC link, supervisory
  limits, matching and protection functions.
- **Removed:** grid-side inverter, AC export chain, electrolyser rectifier and
  separate electrolyser DC/DC stage.
- **Canonical ownership:** shared turbine/electrolyser electrical interface.

## 3. Calculation reconstruction

$P_\mathrm{cmd}$ is the minimum of wind availability, generator capability and
PEM permitted power. The operating current solves
$P_\mathrm{cmd}=V_\mathrm{stack}(I)I$ inside voltage/current limits. Active
module switching changes the equivalent polarisation curve. This structure is
valid, but none of the project-specific envelopes required to solve it exists.

### Boundary and limiting-case checks

- No feasible root must cause curtailment/safe shutdown, not extrapolation.
- Boost-only topology needs an explicit voltage inequality over all speeds.
- Switching introduces discrete capacity states.
- Fault, stale-signal and isolation states require deterministic outputs.
- Energy must be conserved from aerodynamic/generator output through retained
  conversion losses.

## 5. Technical correctness

The principle is presented conditionally and the page distinguishes proof of
principle from multi-MW validation. The final claim should remain conditional
until the voltage/current envelope is shown. The 1.025 multiplier in the
legacy implementation is technically inconsistent with this boundary unless
it is re-expressed as removal of a clearly defined baseline loss upstream.

## 6. Model fidelity

The conceptual fidelity is appropriate. A full electromagnetic model is not
needed for screening; an explicit piecewise efficiency/capability envelope,
equipment ledger and infeasibility gate are.

## 7. Cost and evidence audit

The cited studies support feasibility concepts and function allocation, not
the cost, mass, loss or MW-scale envelope of the selected design. No numerical
direct-DC cost case is currently auditable.

## 8. Independent-implementer test

- **Can the operating point be implemented?** No numerical polarisation or
  generator/converter envelopes.
- **Can CAPEX/mass be implemented?** No.
- **Missing assumptions:** topology, isolation, protection, converter range,
  efficiency, component mass/cost and failure treatment.

## 9. Explanation and site placement

Clear and appropriately detailed. It should be linked from H2P-001 and jointly
owned with TURB-002 through one interface ledger.
